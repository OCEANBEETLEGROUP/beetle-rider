Want to know when Kamakura's coastline is at its most beautiful? The hour before sunset. The bay turns orange, Enoshima becomes a silhouette, and on clear days Mt. Fuji appears behind it — right after most tourists have boarded their trains home.

## Why 3 PM Is the Right Start

You don't need a full day. Our favorite version of this is the **2-hour plan leaving the garage at 3 PM** ([QUICK RIDE, ¥3,500](/en/ebike/)). It works as a "one more ride" after the Buddha and the shrines, or as the whole plan on a day you arrive in Kamakura after lunch.

## The Route

1. **Garage → Yuigahama Beach (5 min)** — get to the water first. At this hour the light comes in sideways and the sand goes gold
2. **West along the coast to Inamuragasaki (10 min)** — the classic lookout park. The Enoshima-and-Fuji two-shot from here is the Kamakura postcard
3. **Shichirigahama → Enoshima (15 min)** — straight down the coastline. On an e-bike, a headwind never kills your legs
4. **Wait for sunset at the Enoshima bridge** — the entrance of Benten Bridge is the best seat. You don't even need to cross to the island

## The Ride Home Is the Highlight

The fifteen minutes back along Route 134 — street lights flickering on over the water — is secretly the best part of this route. Lights on, keep left, take it slow ([riding rules here](/en/ebike-rules/)).

Sunset time swings widely with the seasons. Check it before you book, and count back two hours.
